Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my data stored?
    Everything stays on your device. Wellness Quest has no accounts, no servers, and no tracking — your journal entries, XP, and streaks never leave your phone.
  • Do I need an internet connection?
    No. The app works fully offline after install.
  • How do I reset my data?
    Go to Settings → "Reset Data". This permanently clears all tasks, XP, streaks, and journal entries.
  • Can I customize my tasks?
    Yes. Settings lets you add, remove, and toggle tasks for each of the six wellness categories.
  • When does the weekly recap appear?
    Every Sunday, the app generates a summary of your week: XP earned, best day, streaks, and insights.
